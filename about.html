About ABTech

Our History

ABTech was born in 2021 with a simple dream — to create technology that empowers people and transforms societies. Founded by Bazeet Akande, it quickly grew from a small innovation hub into a globally recognized tech company. Today, ABTech leads in AI, digital transformation, and sustainable innovation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower the next generation through innovative and inclusive technologies that shape a smarter, safer, and sustainable world.

Our Vision

To be a global leader in technology — bridging creativity and artificial intelligence while ensuring excellence, ethics, and equity guide our every move.

Why Choose ABTech

ABTech stands out through innovation, precision, and trust. Our diverse team uses AI, data, and design thinking to build solutions that redefine excellence.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does ABTech specialize in?

We specialize in AI, web/app development, cloud computing, and tech innovation.

Do you collaborate with other companies?

Yes, we partner globally with startups and organizations to deliver joint innovations.

Our main headquarters are in Nigeria, but we operate globally through digital hubs.
