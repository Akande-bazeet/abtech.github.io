About Our Visionary CEO

Bazeet Akande – Founder & CEO of ABTech

Bazeet Akande is a visionary leader who has revolutionized how technology intersects with human potential. With a deep passion for AI, cybersecurity, and innovation, he founded ABTech to bridge the gap between imagination and intelligent reality. Under his leadership, ABTech has grown from a local startup to a globally recognized tech powerhouse.

Driven by the belief that technology should empower, not replace humanity, Bazeet continues to inspire a new generation of thinkers, developers, and dreamers to use tech for global good.

“Technology is not just about innovation — it’s about impact, empowerment, and integrity.”
— Bazeet Akande
