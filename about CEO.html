About ABTech

Our History

ABTech began as a small but ambitious tech startup in 2021, founded by a group of passionate innovators led by Bazeet Akande. What started as an idea to make technology more human-centered soon evolved into a mission to revolutionize the African tech space. Over the years, ABTech expanded into AI research, software development, and smart solutions, gaining global recognition for its innovation, integrity, and impact.

Today, ABTech stands as a beacon of progress — empowering individuals, businesses, and communities through technology that inspires change and fosters growth.

Our Mission

To empower the next generation through innovative and inclusive technologies that shape a smarter, safer, and more sustainable world.

Our Vision

To become a global leader in tech innovation — bridging the gap between creativity and artificial intelligence, while upholding the values of excellence, ethics, and equity.

Why Choose ABTech

At ABTech, we don’t just build solutions — we build trust. Our team combines creativity, experience, and cutting-edge technology to deliver results that exceed expectations.

We’re driven by impact, inspired by innovation, and guided by integrity. Whether it's AI systems, software development, or digital branding — we make technology work for you.
